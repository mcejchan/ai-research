title: "Treat final acceptance notes as durable artifacts"
date: 2026-08-11
category: tooling
component: general
tags: [acceptance, checkpoints, verification, evidence]
---

A fully implemented and passing change remained incomplete because its required final acceptance note was missing. The follow-up required no code repair; it reconstructed the changed-file inventory, behavior summary, exact verification commands, outcomes, and deferred migration decision in a task checkpoint. Reuse this pattern by treating final notes and checkpoints as first-class deliverables, not optional chat output.
